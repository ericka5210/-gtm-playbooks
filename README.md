# Global Brand Campaign GTM Launch Framework
### Template by Ericka May | Senior Program Manager & Integrated Marketing Strategist

---

## Overview

This framework outlines the end-to-end GTM process for a large-scale global brand campaign or rebrand targeting a community-driven audience. It covers the full launch lifecycle from strategy alignment through post-launch measurement.

**Launch Type:** Global Brand Campaign / Rebrand  
**Scope:** Cross-functional, Multi-region  
**Primary Goal:** Build Brand Awareness  
**Industry:** Community & Entertainment  

---

## Phase 1: Strategy & Alignment (Weeks 1–3)

### Goals
- Lock the brand story, visual identity, and campaign message
- Align all stakeholders on goals, budget, and timeline
- Define success metrics before anything is built

### Key Activities

| Activity | Owner | Stakeholders | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand brief finalized | Brand Lead | Creative, Marketing, Legal | Week 1 |
| Campaign goals & KPIs set | Program Manager | Leadership, Analytics | Week 1 |
| Budget approved | Finance | PMO, Marketing Lead | Week 2 |
| Regional leads identified | PMO | AMER, EMEA, APAC leads | Week 2 |
| Legal / trademark review | Legal | Brand, Creative | Week 3 |
| Executive sign-off | PMO | C-Suite | Week 3 |

### KPIs to Define at This Stage
- Brand awareness lift (pre/post survey)
- Social reach and impressions target
- Community growth target (new members, followers)
- Press / earned media target
- Regional engagement benchmarks

---

## Phase 2: Creative Development (Weeks 4–8)

### Goals
- Develop all campaign creative assets
- Build regional variations where needed
- Ensure brand consistency across every touchpoint

### Key Activities

| Activity | Owner | Stakeholders | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative concept presented | Creative Director | Brand, Marketing | Week 4 |
| Concept approved | Brand Lead | Executive team | Week 5 |
| Visual identity locked | Creative | Brand, Legal | Week 5 |
| Asset production begins | Creative | Copy, Design, Video | Week 5 |
| Regional adaptations briefed | Regional Leads | Creative, Local marketing | Week 6 |
| First asset review | PMO | Brand, Creative, Marketing | Week 7 |
| Final assets delivered | Creative | All teams | Week 8 |

### Asset Checklist
- [ ] Hero campaign video
- [ ] Social media toolkit (IG, TikTok, Twitter/X, YouTube)
- [ ] Press kit and media assets
- [ ] Community landing page / microsite copy
- [ ] Email templates
- [ ] Regional variations (language, cultural adaptation)
- [ ] Influencer / ambassador briefing deck
- [ ] Internal launch announcement

---

## Phase 3: Go-To-Market Planning (Weeks 6–10)

### Goals
- Build the channel strategy and distribution plan
- Confirm partnerships, influencers, and press
- Align every team on launch day execution

### Channel Strategy

| Channel | Tactic | Owner | Region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social Media | Organic + paid campaign rollout | Social team | Global |
| Influencer / Ambassador | Seeding + content partnerships | Partnerships | AMER, EMEA, APAC |
| Press & PR | Embargo outreach, launch story | PR lead | Global |
| Community Platforms | Announcement + member activation | Community Manager | Global |
| Email | Member and subscriber campaign | CRM / Email team | Global |
| Events / Activations | Launch event or virtual moment | Events | TBD by region |
| Paid Media | Awareness campaign (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) | Media team | Global |

### Key Activities

| Activity | Owner | Stakeholders | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Channel plan finalized | Marketing Lead | PMO, Creative | Week 6 |
| Media buy confirmed | Media team | Finance, Marketing | Week 7 |
| Influencer contracts signed | Partnerships | Legal, Brand | Week 8 |
| Press embargo list confirmed | PR | Brand, Legal | Week 8 |
| Launch day runbook drafted | PMO | All leads | Week 9 |
| Regional activation plans confirmed | Regional Leads | PMO | Week 9 |
| Internal comms plan sent | PMO | All teams | Week 10 |

---

## Phase 4: Pre-Launch (Weeks 10–11)

### Goals
- Final checks across every workstream
- Team aligned on launch day roles and timing
- Nothing is a surprise on launch day

### Launch Readiness Checklist

**Creative & Content**
- [ ] All assets final and approved
- [ ] All regional adaptations complete
- [ ] Website / landing page live in staging, tested
- [ ] Social posts scheduled and QA'd

**Partnerships & PR**
- [ ] Influencer content reviewed and approved
- [ ] Press embargo confirmed with outlets
- [ ] Ambassador briefing complete

**Operations**
- [ ] Launch day runbook distributed to all leads
- [ ] Time zones mapped for global rollout sequence
- [ ] Escalation path confirmed (who calls who if something breaks)
- [ ] Community moderation plan in place
- [ ] Analytics tracking confirmed and tested

**Internal**
- [ ] Leadership briefed
- [ ] All-hands or launch team sync completed
- [ ] Customer support / community team briefed on FAQs

## Phase 5: Launch Day Execution

### Global Rollout Sequence

| Time (ET) | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 6:00 AM | APAC activation goes live | APAC Regional Lead |
| 8:00 AM | Press embargo lifts | PR Lead |
| 9:00 AM | EMEA social and paid goes live | EMEA Regional Lead |
| 10:00 AM | AMER launch — hero content drops | Social / Marketing Lead |
| 10:00 AM | Email send to full list | CRM team |
| 10:15 AM | Influencer content goes live | Partnerships |
| 10:30 AM | Community announcement posted | Community Manager |
| All day | Real-time monitoring & response | Social + Community teams |
| 5:00 PM | Day 1 performance check | PMO + Analytics |

### War Room Setup
- Dedicated Slack channel for launch day comms: `#launch-day-[campaign-name]`
- Hourly check-ins between regional leads for first 6 hours
- Live dashboard tracking impressions, reach, community growth, and press pickups
- One PMO on point for escalations all day

---

## Phase 6: Post-Launch & Measurement (Weeks 12–16)

### Goals
- Measure performance against KPIs
- Capture learnings while they're fresh
- Sustain momentum beyond launch week

### 30-Day Measurement Plan

| Metric | Target | Measurement Tool | Review Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand awareness lift | +X% vs baseline | Pre/post survey | 30 days post-launch |
| Social impressions | X million | Native analytics + Sprout | Weekly |
| Community growth | +X new members | Community platform | Weekly |
| Press pickups | X articles | PR tool / manual tracking | Weekly |
| Influencer reach | X million | Partnerships report | Week 2 |
| Paid media performance | X CPM / CTR | Media dashboard | Daily week 1, weekly after |
| Email open rate | X% | CRM platform | Day 3 |

### Post-Launch Activities

| Activity | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Day 7 performance report | Analytics + PMO | Week 1 post-launch |
| Influencer wrap report | Partnerships | Week 2 post-launch |
| Press coverage roundup | PR | Week 2 post-launch |
| Regional performance review | Regional Leads + PMO | Week 3 post-launch |
| Full campaign retrospective | PMO | Week 4 post-launch |
| Learnings documented | PMO | Week 4 post-launch |

## RACI Matrix

| Workstream | Program Manager | Brand Lead | Creative | Marketing | Regional Leads | Legal | Finance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy & KPIs | A | R | C | C | I | C | C |
| Budget | A | C | I | C | I | I | R |
| Creative Development | I | A | R | C | C | C | I |
| Channel Planning | A | C | I | R | C | I | I |
| Regional Activation | A | I | C | C | R | I | I |
| Launch Day Execution | R | C | I | C | C | I | I |
| Measurement & Reporting | R | C | I | C | C | I | I |

**R** = Responsible | **A** = Accountable | **C** = Consulted | **I** = Informed

---

## Escalation Path

| Issue | First Contact | Escalate To |
|---|---|---|
| Creative not approved | Brand Lead | CMO |
| Regional activation blocked | Regional Lead | PMO |
| Legal hold | Legal | General Counsel |
| Tech / site issue on launch day | Engineering Lead | CTO |
| Press crisis | PR Lead | CMO + Legal |
| Budget overrun | PMO | CFO |
